# Code Of Conduct

This project should be a welcoming place for people learning Python, Magic deckbuilding, open-source collaboration, or all three at once.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially from beginners.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect privacy. Do not pressure people to share private collection files.
- Avoid gatekeeping around deck power level, budget, operating system, editor, or AI tooling.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private data.
- Mocking beginner questions.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes the project feel unsafe or unwelcoming, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub. If the issue involves private information, do not include that information in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block users when needed to keep the project healthy.
